## Your pictures stay on your device

Every photo, video or GIF you open in the editor or the small apps (profile picture, colour splash,
make it move, fancy text) is read and filtered **in your browser**. It is not uploaded to us or to
anyone else. The same goes for the selection tools: "find objects" runs its neural network (MobileSAM)
in your browser, from a file our site serves; what it sees is never sent anywhere. What you download
is made on your device too.

## What is stored on your device

The site keeps a few things in your browser's local storage, so they are there next time. They stay on
your device; we never receive them. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes them.

- filters you save in the editor ("Your saved filters");
- your choices: which panels are open, and which object model to use.

There are **no cookies** and **no analytics or advertising** on qblur.

## Share links

A link you copy carries the filter itself after the `#` in the address. Browsers don't send that part
to the server, so when someone opens your link, the filter goes from the link to their browser, not
through us. A link carries the filter, never the picture.
