Image filters

Guide

Write a filter as maths, give it knobs and notes, save it, and share it as a link anyone can open, change and keep as their own. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
Just want one thing done? Profile picture · Colour splash · Make it move · Fancy text

A filter is maths run on every pixel: r g b a the pixel, x y its position (0–1), px py in pixels, t the frame, gray its brightness, pixel(px, py) another pixel. Chain filters, tune their knobs, and share the result as a link — anyone can fork it. See the guide.
