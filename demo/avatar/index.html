Profile picture

Pick a photo of yourself. It's centred on you and cut to a circle; choose a style and a background — or none, for a cut-out sticker — drag to adjust, and download. It all happens in your browser — your photo isn't uploaded.

On the photo:

Outline not right? Choose "Mark me" and click on a part of you it missed, or "Mark not me" and click on what it took by mistake — a few points work best. Green dots are on you, red ones not. When part of the outline is right, choose "Keep as is" and paint over it (blue): it stays that way while you fix the rest.
