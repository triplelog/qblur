Image filters written as maths

Write a filter as maths, give it knobs and notes, save it, and share it as a link anyone can open, change and keep as their own. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Try one

A filter is maths run on every pixel: r g b the pixel's colour, x y its position (0–1), px py in pixels, gray its brightness, t the frame. Change the maths and the picture follows.

Open this filter in the editor →

The editor has the rest: GIFs, filters chained with knobs, a library to start from, selecting part of a picture, text and brushes, and downloads at full size. To write and save your own filters, open the filters page.

On your own site

qblur is a JavaScript library: one <script> from a CDN puts these filters — or your own maths — on your pages, live on photos, video or a webcam, with knobs, share links and text. It runs on the GPU, or the CPU where there is none (Node too). Free, MIT. How to use it →
